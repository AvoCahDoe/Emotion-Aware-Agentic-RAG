# Connect NovaAssist to Slack

1. In NovaAssist, open Integrations → Slack → Connect.
2. Approve the requested permissions in Slack’s OAuth screen.
3. Pick the channel where answers should be posted.
4. In Slack, type /nova ask followed by your question.
5. To disconnect, remove the NovaAssist app from Slack or click Disconnect in Integrations.
